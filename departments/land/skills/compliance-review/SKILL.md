# Compliance Review

1. Identify the jurisdiction and version or date of each supplied requirement.
2. Build a requirement matrix with requirement, evidence, source, status, and gap.
3. Check only configured or authoritative case requirements.
4. Mark absent rules, deadlines, forms, fees, and approvals as unknown.
5. Route gaps, conflicts, and interpretation questions to human review.

Do not invent law, approve or reject a transfer, or treat missing configuration
as evidence of compliance.
