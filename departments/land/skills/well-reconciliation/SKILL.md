# Well Reconciliation

1. Confirm the submitted API number, county, well number, and source snapshot.
2. Compare independent normalized well and production evidence.
3. Preserve source disagreement and distinguish no-match from reported zero.
4. Record findings, unknowns, conflicts, provenance, and failure status.
5. Route unresolved identity, operator, production, or title questions to human review.

WVDEP and WVGES remain independent evidence sources. Public well evidence is
not proof of title or ownership.
